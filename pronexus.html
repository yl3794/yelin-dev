ProNexus

As a Software Engineer Intern at ProNexus, I built a two-stage pipeline that helps recruiters find the right candidates faster. It first uses FAISS to quickly pull the closest roles for each of 20,000+ applicants, then ranks them with XGBoost across 3,000+ open roles. I also used AI classification to translate skills into one shared vocabulary, so a résumé that says "ReactJS" matches a job that asks for "React." Finally, I made reprocessing a candidate 80% faster, from 15 seconds to 3, with incremental writes, automatic retries, and rate-limit handling.
