Skill2

Skill2 is an AI movement coach my team built at the Cornell AI Hackathon. MediaPipe tracks your joints through a webcam, and Claude turns that movement data into simple advice about your form. I led backend development and integration: I built the FastAPI service that processes the pose data and sends feedback to our React app, and I agreed on the API details with our frontend teammates early so everything fit together at demo time.
